Foundational Literature

  • Deterministic Nonperiodic Flow
    Edward N. Lorenz • Journal of the Atmospheric Sciences (1963)
    Derives the three-dimensional atmospheric convection ODE system ($\dot{x}=\sigma(y-x)$, $\dot{y}=rx-y-xz$, $\dot{z}=xy-bz$), establishing sensitive dependence on initial conditions in geophysical fluids.
  • The Effect of Solar Radiation Variations on the Climate of the Earth
    Mikhail I. Budyko • Tellus (1969)
    Formulates zonal energy-balance climate equations incorporating ice-albedo feedback and multiple climate equilibria (warm vs. ice-covered states).
  • A Global Climatic Model Based on the Energy Balance of the Earth-Atmosphere System
    William D. Sellers • Journal of Applied Meteorology (1969)
    Couples meridional heat transport diffusion with radiative transfer to quantify global temperature sensitivity to solar and greenhouse perturbations.
  • Principles of Geostatistics
    Georges Matheron • Economic Geology (1963)
    Introduces regionalized variables, variograms, and kriging interpolation for spatial estimation in geology, hydrology, and environmental science.
