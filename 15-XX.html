Algebra • Matrix Theory

Linear and Multilinear Algebra; Matrix Theory

Vector spaces, linear transformations $T: V \to W$, eigenvalues $\det(A - \lambda I) = 0$, matrix factorizations, and multilinear structures.

Core Topics & Subfields

Vector Spaces, Linear Dependence & Rank
Bases, dimension, subspaces, $\dim(\ker T) + \dim(\operatorname{im} T) = \dim V$, and matrix rank.
Linear & Semilinear Transformations
Matrix representations, change of basis, dual spaces $V^*$, and invariant subspaces.
Systems of Linear Equations
Gaussian elimination, row echelon forms, solution spaces of $Ax = b$, and consistency criteria.
Matrix Inversion & Generalized Inverses
Invertibility conditions, block matrix inverses, and Moore–Penrose pseudoinverses $A^+$.
Determinants, Permanents & Traces
Alternating multilinear forms, $\det(AB) = \det(A)\det(B)$, $\operatorname{tr}(A)$, and permanents.
Eigenvalues, Singular Values & Eigenvectors
Characteristic polynomials, algebraic vs. geometric multiplicity, and spectral theorems.
Canonical Forms & Classification
Diagonalization, Jordan normal form, rational canonical form, and similarity invariants.
Matrix Factorizations
Singular Value Decomposition $A = U\Sigma V^T$, QR, LU, Cholesky, and polar decompositions.
Matrix Norms & Numerical Range
Induced norms $\|A\|_p$, Frobenius norm $\|A\|_F$, spectral radius $\rho(A)$, and field of values.
Quadratic & Bilinear Forms; Inner Products
Inner product spaces, Gram–Schmidt orthogonalization, and Sylvester's law of inertia.
Multilinear Algebra & Tensor Products
Tensor products $V \otimes W$, exterior algebra $\Lambda^k(V)$, and multilinear maps.
Special Matrices
Hermitian, unitary, orthogonal, positive definite, stochastic, Toeplitz, and permutation matrices.
