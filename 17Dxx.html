Algebra • Nonassociative Rings

Other Nonassociative Rings and Algebras

Algebraic structures governed by nonvanishing associators $(x, y, z) = (xy)z - x(yz)$, extending beyond associative, Lie, and Jordan frameworks.

Core Topics & Subfields

Alternative Rings and Algebras
Structures satisfying $(x, x, y) = 0$ and $(y, x, x) = 0$, Artin's theorem, octonions $\mathbb{O}$, and Zorn's vector-matrix algebras.
Mal'tsev Rings and Algebras
Anticommutative algebras satisfying $J(x, y, xz) = J(x, y, z)x$, tangent algebras of analytic Moufang loops, and binary Lie algebras.
Right Alternative Rings and Algebras
Rings satisfying the right alternative identity $(yx)x = yx^2$, nuclei, centers, and radical structure.
$(\gamma, \delta)$-Rings and $(1, -1)$-Rings
Nonassociative rings defined by linear associator identities $(x, y, z) + \gamma(y, x, z) + \delta(z, x, y) = 0$.
Lie-Admissible Algebras
Algebras whose commutator $[x, y] = xy - yx$ forms a Lie algebra, including pre-Lie and Vinberg algebras.
Genetic Algebras
Baric, train, and Bernstein algebras modeling Mendelian and non-Mendelian inheritance in population genetics.
Structurable & Composition Algebras
Algebras with involution generalizing Jordan algebras, Cayley–Dickson doublings, and Kantor–Koecher–Tits constructions.
